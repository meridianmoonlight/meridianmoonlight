# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be someone people want to build with.

Vigorous disagreement about technical substance is the entire point of this repository — we are actively asking people to attack our numbers. Contempt for people is a different thing, and it isn't welcome.

## What we're going for

**Attack the claim, not the person.** "This assumption is wrong because X" is exactly what we want. "Only an idiot would assume X" is the same information with the useful part removed.

**Assume competence and good faith.** Contributors come from different specialisms. Someone who doesn't know how Android foreground services work may be the person who catches a two-order-of-magnitude error in the compute model.

**Being wrong is not a status loss here.** This project retracted its own headline claim in its own whitepaper. Anyone who treats a corrected mistake as an opportunity to score points is undermining the thing that makes the project work.

**Be patient with newcomers.** Especially people bringing a measurement from their phone rather than a pull request. That table of measured device throughput is one of the most valuable artefacts this project will produce, and the people building it are not necessarily engineers.

**Respect the participant.** Discussion about design choices affecting people's devices, batteries, and legal exposure gets treated seriously. "Users won't notice" and "nobody actually reads that" are not arguments.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, intimidation, or sustained hostility, in issues, PRs, commits, or anywhere the project's name is on it
- Demeaning comments related to age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience level, education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity or orientation
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Deliberately derailing discussions or exhausting people through repetition
- Advocating for or encouraging any of the above

## Scope

Applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and the project's public communication channels — and when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report to **conduct@meridianmoonlight.com**. Reports are handled confidentially. You will get an acknowledgement within 72 hours.

Responses, roughly in escalation order: a private word, a public correction, a warning, temporary interaction limits, permanent ban. Which one depends on severity, pattern, and whether the person course-corrects.

**Currently one person handles these reports**, which is a real limitation — if your report concerns that person, or you'd rather not go to them, say so and we will find a neutral third party. Broadening this is part of the [governance work at M3](docs/governance.md#contributor-governance).
